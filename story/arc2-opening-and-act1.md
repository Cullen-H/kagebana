# Arc II draft script: opening and Act I

**Status: draft for your approval. None of this is in the game yet.**

The rift entrance and tag-team fight before this are already built. Lines marked `(H)` are spoken by whichever hero you're playing, written so they work for anyone.

Sekka and Kuroto are written here for "the one you picked" and "the one you didn't". Where it matters, both versions are shown.

---

## Opening: the rift settles

*Back at the ruined shrine, some runs after the fight. The rift has stopped growling.*

**Sekka:** It has settled. We can cross.
**Kuroto:** You look terrible.
**Sekka:** I always look like this.
**Kuroto:** No. You don't.
**(H):** Is it safe?
**Sekka:** It will not grind you to paste. That is the most anyone can promise.
**Kuroto:** Stay near me when we land. The first thing you'll want to do over there is stand still and look. Don't.

---

## Arriving in Tokoyo

*The snow doesn't fall. It hangs. Every flake sits where it stopped. A festival fills the street, and nobody in it is moving.*

**(H):** ...Are they dead?
**Sekka:** No. It is still the same night here. It has been the same night for a very long time.
**(H):** Can they hear us?
**Sekka:** I used to ask them that.
**Kuroto:** Don't touch the snow. It's still exactly as hard as whatever hit it last.

*Further in, a frozen vendor twitches. Something dark moves behind their eyes. The vendor's head turns, and their smile doesn't change.*

**(H):** You said nobody moves here!
**Kuroto:** Nobody does. That's not somebody. Those are the Worn. That thing you killed at the bottom left its filth in every crack in this place. It climbs inside them and walks them around.
**Sekka:** They can be freed. The same way as your gods.
**Kuroto:** By hitting them until it leaves.
**Sekka:** That is what I said.

## Hub: the first steps back
*Between runs, once Tokoyo has opened up.*

**Hub, if you picked Sekka:**
**Sekka:** You walk into that street as if you expect to come back.
**(H):** I do come back. It's the one thing I'm good at.
**Sekka:** ...Yes. I suppose it is.

**Hub, if you picked Kuroto:**
**Kuroto:** She's getting slower.
**(H):** You sound worried.
**Kuroto:** I sound like a man counting.

## Boss: Gekkou, Unbroken

*At the top of the festival street, a swordsman stands with his back to you. White hair, a plain coat, a sprig of plum tied to his scabbard. He is smiling at something. He isn't Hollow and he isn't masked. He looks like he did before anything went wrong.*

**(H), if playing Akane:** ...Master?
**(H), otherwise:** Is that Gekkou? The one from the grove?
**Sekka:** In your world, he walked into the bamboo to save his village and never walked out. Here, he was on his way to buy a student a sweet. It is still that evening.
*The smile stays exactly the same. His head turns.*
**Gekkou:** Such a fine night. Such a fine night. Such a fine night.
**Kuroto:** It's worn him a long time. It knows everything he knows. Don't let him draw.

**First phase change. Gekkou:** I remember every step of this road. I remember every step. I remember —
**Second phase change. Gekkou:** Let me finish. Let me finish. She is waiting. Let me buy the —

**Defeat:**
*The dark pours out of him and soaks into the cracks. Gekkou is frozen again, still smiling, sprig of plum in place. But his hand has stopped halfway to his blade, and he's looking at you now.*
**(H):** He can see us?
**Sekka:** No. ...No. It is only the angle.
**Kuroto:** *(looks at her and says nothing)*

*In Hinomoto, Gekkou's fragment hint read "Help Gekkou remember who he was." This Gekkou has the opposite problem, and that's the point of this boss.*

---

## Rescue: Ponta

*Behind the festival stage, a rack of frozen lanterns. Between them, one small and very much unfrozen tanuki is hammering at the Worn's discarded armor with a stolen ladle.*

**Ponta:** — and ANOTHER thing, you've got the temper all wrong on this, who forged this, a child? Moonbound! MOONBOUND! Did you know they don't have tea here?!
**(H):** Ponta! You're alive!
**Ponta:** I'm extremely alive! It's the only thing happening for miles! Everything's stopped, the coals won't catch, the water won't pour, I've been shouting at a frozen man for what I think is a week!
**(H):** Have you seen the others?
**Ponta:** Heard them. Something's got them further up. Tall place. Lots of iron. ...Who's that?
**Kuroto:** Nobody.
**Ponta:** Well, nobody's got a bad grip on that spear. I can fix that. Get me back to my forge.

*Back at the ruined shrine, the forge is relit and Ponta stands at it again. The first piece of the shrine has come back.*

**Unlock:** the one you didn't pick becomes playable, and Ponta's forge returns.

**Hanabi tease at the end of Act I.** *A faint voice carries down the street:* "...and I SAID, this is NOT how you fold an aburaage..."
**Sekka:** Your fox's clock is still ticking.
**(H):** Is that —
**Kuroto:** Long way up. Move.

## Things to weigh in on

1. **Gekkou's "Such a fine night" loop:** creepy in the right way for you, or too much?
2. **Kuroto says "Nobody" when Ponta asks who he is:** I like this as the first small hint about his fate. It's easy to cut if it's too on the nose.
3. **Ponta's tone:** the original game plays Ponta for comedy, so I kept that here as relief after the boss. Does it fit, coming right after a frozen Gekkou?
4. **Length:** this runs slightly longer than the game's existing scenes. I can trim it if you want it snappier.
