# Kagebana Arc II: story plan

**Status: agreed direction. The rift entrance, tag-team fight and Act I are built. Everything after that is still to build.**

Names and characters marked *(new)* are proposals and easy to change.

## The big idea
The rift doesn't lead to a mirror of Akane's world. It leads to **Tokoyo (常世)**, a different world with its own cast, its own gods and its own broken moon. **Sekka and Kuroto are the leads of its story, the way Akane is of hers.** Other worlds exist beyond it, kept for later arcs.

Akane isn't the main character here. For three campaigns she was the chosen one. In Tokoyo she's a stranger in someone else's story.

## How power works
**Every world has its own moon, its own shard power and its own gods.**

| | Hinomoto | Tokoyo |
|---|---|---|
| **Moon** | Moon of Memory | Moon of Time *(new)* |
| **Shard power** | Coming back from death, paid for in memories | Holding a moment still, paid for with your own life |
| **Who has it** | Akane, and through her anyone who fights beside her | Sekka, the keeper of its clock shrine |
| **Gods** | The seven Kami, now free | Tokoyo's own gods, frozen with everything else |

**Respawning:** coming back is Akane's shard power. Everyone else the player uses is tied to her along a thinner thread, so they come back too and pay the same price. Already in the game: Rei's first talk and the Moonbound Codex entry.

**Why Akane's Kami still work in Tokoyo:** they can't walk there, but they poured their power into her, and her shard is the connection. Their blessings travel along it. She is the one link between the two worlds' powers.

**New gods:** Tokoyo's gods are frozen. Freeing one gives a new set of boons, smaller than the Kami's, themed around time and iron. Two gods to start.

**Kuroto:** he has zero spirit, so no power system can see him. Sekka's stop can't hold him, gods can't bless him, and the Calamity can't wear him.

**So how does Kuroto respawn?** Akane's shard works on memory, and the moon has no record of him, so the shard can't find him on its own. He comes back only because Akane personally remembers him. If she ever forgot him, he wouldn't come back. That makes the arc's ending matter in gameplay terms too.

## Why Sekka matters
**She caused everything, and she did it out of love.**

- She says time stopped in her world. The truth is that she stopped it.
- She was keeper of a clock shrine. The night the moon broke, she saw a shard falling at her best friend, **Kohaku** *(new; her name means amber)*, and stopped time one second before it hit.
- Kohaku stands frozen with the shard an inch from her heart.
- Holding that second is killing Sekka, and her lines about the rift's "fever" are really about herself.
- The stop also pins the Calamity's true body, caught halfway out of a crack. It can't be killed until she lets go.
- **Why she needs Akane:** Sekka has only ever imagined what the shard would do to Kohaku. Akane is the living answer, a girl who took a shard and lived a thousand deaths. The question Sekka finally asks is the emotional center of the arc: **"Was it worth it?"**

## Why Kuroto matters
**He is the one person in Tokoyo who didn't stop, and he had to watch.**

- Born with zero spirit into the Amagiri, a hunter clan, who treated him as defective.
- When Sekka stopped time, the stop passed straight through him, because no power system can see him. He woke up in a silent world with everyone he knew frozen, including the few people who were ever kind to him. One is **Tsubame** *(new)*, a younger clan hunter who scratched his name into her gauntlet as a joke.
- He spent years working out who did it, then training with nothing but his body to kill her. That's why he's an assassin, and why he keeps attacking her.
- **Sekka can't stop him, and she can't hear him.** She hears everyone's clock, and he doesn't have one. So when she says "I am the only one who still moves," she means it, even standing next to him. Her time stop does nothing to him, so every fight has been real.
- **What neither says aloud:** those fights are the only thing that has happened to either of them in years. He could have ended it by now. He keeps not doing it.
- **The cost:** when the moon is whole, its record takes over, and he was never in it. Everyone he stayed awake for will forget him the moment they wake up.

## Why the fight at the rift matters
Each of them is telling the truth. She's holding a world hostage to save one person, and he's trying to kill the only one who can find your friends. Your first choice is whose side you believe, before you know the whole story.

## The story

### Opening: the rift settles
Sekka says it's safe. Kuroto notices she looks worse.

### Act I: Town of Hanging Snow
- The frozen festival. First sight of the Worn: the Calamity's filth crawls into frozen people and walks them around. Slaying one frees that person, the same rule as the corrupted gods. Sekka knows every frozen person's name.
- **Boss: Okina, Master of the Festival** *(new)*, the town's beloved festival master in a smiling Noh mask, stuck repeating "Such a fine night."
- **Rescue: Ponta.** The forge returns to the ruined shrine. The newcomer you didn't pick becomes playable.

### Act II: The Iron Keep of the Amagiri
- Kuroto's home, frozen mid-ceremony. He walks through it without looking at anyone, then stops at one person: Tsubame, mid-laugh, with his name scratched into her gauntlet.
- **Boss: Amagiri Genba** *(new)*, the clan head who cast him out, worn by the Calamity. Kuroto won't say whether killing him felt good.
- **Rescue: Obaa Tama.** The lanterns relight.

### Act III: The Clock Shrine of Tokimiya
- **The reveal:** thousands of stopped clocks, all at the same second, and one watch missing from its case. The player sees Kohaku frozen in the distance. The truth comes out: Sekka did this.
- **Boss: a rematch against whichever newcomer you didn't pick,** for real this time and with the truth out. If you picked Kuroto, you fight Sekka in stopped time. If you picked Sekka, you fight Kuroto.
- It ends in stalemate and a new deal: finish it properly, together.
- **Rescue: Sanzu and the heroes.** The shrine is nearly whole.

### Act IV: The Held Second
- A climb into the frozen sky through hanging shards, and it gets harder as Sekka weakens.
- At the top are Kohaku and **Hanabi**, who the Calamity kept to wear as a way to reach Akane. Hanabi is awake, annoyed and complaining about tofu, as the lore promised.
- **Boss: Ōmagatsuhi, True Body.**
  1. **Frozen:** it can't be hurt. Only Kuroto can reach it and crack it.
  2. **Sekka asks Akane, "Was it worth it?"** Akane answers, and Sekka lets go. Time crashes back on a whole world at once.
  3. **It strips your boons and names,** as it promised ("I will wear YOU"). Kuroto is the one thing it can't take.

### Ending
- The second finishes and the shard strikes Kohaku. She will die and return, but she won't walk that road blind, because she'll have Akane and Sekka. Sekka finally sees what comes after the second she's been holding, and lives it, weakened.
- Tokoyo wakes. Tsubame finishes her laugh and looks at a grown stranger, then at a name scratched into her gauntlet. She doesn't know whose it is, but she doesn't polish it out.
- Kuroto walks off.
- In Hinomoto, his Codex page has read "The moon has no record of this person" throughout. It's now filled in by hand, by Hanabi, from what Akane told them. Memory is something people do for each other, not something a moon does.
- Hanabi's cut-off line, "Promise you'll —", is finally finished: "...bring fried tofu."

## Running gag that turns sad
Everyone at the restored shrine keeps forgetting Kuroto. Ponta asks who he is. Then Ponta asks again, a few runs later. Tama introduces herself to him twice. It's funny until the player realizes why it's happening, and only the player's hero always remembers him.

## What would actually change in the game

### Story and content
- A fourth campaign with 4 acts, new music tracks and new enemies.
- New bosses: Okina, Genba, the rematch and the True Body.
- The ruined shrine rebuilds itself piece by piece as you rescue people.
- Two new gods, with new boons.
- **New characters:** Tsubame and Kohaku are the natural playable heroes.

### New features
**Shard Assist** *(your idea; fits the lore well)*
- Pick one other hero as your assist before a run. Press a button mid-fight, and they appear beside you, do one move, and leave, on a cooldown.
- **Lore:** Akane's thread already ties her allies to her, so this is that thread pulling someone through for a moment.
- **Unlock:** Ponta suggests it after being rescued in Act I ("You fought better with someone next to you"). That's also the natural point in the story, right after the tag-team fight.
- **Building it:** the tag attacks already built are the base. The work is one short move for each of the 20+ heroes, so I'd start with a handful and add the rest over time.
- **Rule to decide:** Kuroto as an assist would be the only one not on the thread. A small line about it would be a nice touch.

**Differently shaped rooms** *(your idea)*
- Today every room is a plain rectangle, and the walls, spawn points and enemy movement all assume that.
- **Recommended:** add solid wall blocks inside the rectangle, which gives L-shapes, corridors and pillars with little risk. Enemies walk straight at you and don't path-find, so layouts need to be open enough that they don't get stuck, or they'll need simple pathing.

**Interactive environments** *(your idea)*
- Explosive barrels, and things themed to each act: stopped lanterns that burst when hit, hanging ice that drops when shot, forge bellows in the Iron Keep.
- These work well with Kuroto's Grab Throw, which can throw a foe into one.
- Mostly new content on top of existing explosion code, so this is low risk.

### Already done
- Kuroto's special now changes with his weapon.
- Sekka's knives hit harder (8 to 10 damage each).
- Rinne's landed parries have no cooldown.
- Respawning now comes from Akane's shard in the game's lore.
- Kuroto is from Sekka's world in the rift scene.

## Build order
1. **Act I (done, in `js/tokoyo.js`):** rift becomes a real gate to a fourth campaign, the first biome, the Worn, Okina, the script, Ponta's rescue. Story beats fire only once, since players replay acts.
2. **Shard Assist (done, in `js/assist.js`),** because it unlocks at the end of Act I. Akane, Rei, Yuki, Kaito, Sekka and Kuroto have their own moves; everyone else uses Thread Strike until they get one.
3. **Rooms and hazards,** added to Act I so they can be tested in play.
4. **Acts II and III,** then the new gods.
5. **Act IV and the ending.**

## Originality
- The new world and cast are original, and they help give Sekka and Kuroto identities of their own.
- Before any public release, their kits still need changing: Sekka resembles Sakuya Izayoi (Touhou), and "Toki yo Tomare" is a JoJo's catchphrase. Kuroto resembles Toji Fushiguro (Jujutsu Kaisen), even more so now that he's a zero-power assassin outcast from a clan. Other characters should get the same check.
